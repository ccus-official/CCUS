Help Center

💬

CCUS Official Support

For help and support, contact CCUS Official Support on Telegram.

How can I recharge?

Open Wallet, select Deposit, choose a recharge amount and follow the payment instructions.

When does my recharge appear?

Recharge requests remain Pending until they are reviewed and approved by Admin.

Does a pending recharge increase my balance?

No. A pending recharge does not change Total Balance or Total Recharge.

What happens after approval?

After Admin approval, the approved recharge amount is added to Total Recharge. A recharge does not directly increase Total Balance.

How do referral commissions work?

When a referred user makes an approved recharge, the referral commission is determined by the deposit level configured by CCUS Admin. Commission amounts are fixed ETB values, not percentages.

How do daily tasks work?

Daily tasks are available from Monday through Saturday for eligible users. The number of available tasks and reward per task are configured by CCUS Admin. Each task can be claimed only once per day.

Is Sunday a task day?

No. Sunday is a Rest Day. Tasks cannot be claimed on Sunday.

How does withdrawal work?

Select an amount, enter your password and submit the withdrawal request. The requested amount is reserved from Total Balance and the request remains Pending until Admin reviews it.

What happens if Admin rejects my withdrawal?

If a withdrawal is rejected, the reserved amount should be returned to your Total Balance.

About CCUS

CCUS is a digital earning and task-management platform designed to provide users with digital tasks, referral opportunities, rewards and financial transaction features.

CCUS Balance System

Total Balance represents earnings such as task rewards, rewards, daily income and approved referral commissions.

Total Recharge represents the user's own approved recharge amount.

Important

Recharge requests are reviewed by CCUS Admin. A pending recharge does not increase your Total Recharge until it is approved.
